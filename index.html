个人简介 · Short Profile

Dr. Hao Wen is currently a ZHIZHEN Postdoctoral Fellow at South China University of Technology, where his research focuses on intelligent operation and maintenance technologies for offshore wind farms. He obtained his Ph.D. in Marine Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, M.S. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering from Ocean University of China, and B.S. in Mechatronic Engineering from Northeast Forestry University. His interdisciplinary training integrates expertise in mechatronics, control theory, and marine engineering.

文浩博士现任华南理工大学“至臻”博士后研究员，研究方向为海上风电智能运维技术。 获上海交通大学海洋技术博士学位、中国海洋大学船舶与海洋工程硕士学位、 东北林业大学机械电子工程学士学位。学科背景涵盖机电一体化、控制理论与海洋工程。

He has established a strong publication record, with 23 SCI-indexed peer-reviewed articles (including 14 as first or corresponding author) published in internationally recognised journals such as Energy, Ocean Engineering, and IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering. His research is a continuous program on cooperative control of heterogeneous multi-body marine unmanned systems, running from his doctoral thesis through his current projects to the proposed CO-DOCK fellowship.

在 Energy、Ocean Engineering、IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering 等国际权威期刊发表SCI论文23篇（其中第一/通讯作者14篇）。 研究主线为异构多体海洋无人系统协同控制，贯穿博士课题、在研项目及后续研究计划。

He has participated as a core researcher in multiple competitive projects spanning underwater robotics, deep-sea mining systems, marine research-fleet evaluation, and offshore wind operation and maintenance. He is a three-time recipient of the National Scholarship of China (2020, 2022, 2023), and was awarded Outstanding Graduate of Shanghai (2025), Outstanding Graduate of Shandong Province (2020), and the Outstanding Dissertation Award of Shandong Province (2021).

作为核心研究人员参与水下机器人、深海采矿、海洋科考船队评估及海上风电运维等 多个竞争性科研项目。三次荣获国家奖学金（2020、2022、2023），获评上海市优秀毕业生（2025）、 山东省优秀毕业生（2020）及山东省优秀硕士学位论文（2021）。
